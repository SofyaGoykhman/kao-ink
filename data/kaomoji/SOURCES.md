# Kaomoji sources

Reviewed 2026-10-08. Policy: use only sources with an explicit permissive license
(CC0, MIT, CC BY, Unlicense, public domain) **and** no stated provenance from a
commercial or unlicensed kaomoji website. Rejected: no license, NC/ND terms,
copyleft-only data, and "MIT-labelled" scrapes of third-party sites (a repo owner
cannot relicense content they scraped). Kaomoji websites themselves
(japaneseemoticons, kaomoji.ru, kaomoji.info, kaomojiya, kaosute, textfac.es, …)
were not used as sources, even when they look free.

Raw files are downloaded by `build.mjs` from pinned commits into
`data/raw/kaomoji/<id>/` (gitignored).

portions". Ship this text (e.g. on an /about or /licenses page and in the repo):

> [asciilib](https://github.com/iansinnott/asciilib) © 2022 Ian Sinnott (MIT License).

The full MIT texts are in `data/raw/kaomoji/*/LICENSE` after a build; they should
be copied into the site's licenses page by the lead.

## Parts (`parts.json`)

All constructor parts are original selections of single glyphs and short glyph
pairs (`source: original`); no third-party part list was copied. Generators
reviewed but not used: xxczaki/oji (MIT), keithdowd/kaomoji (MIT), w33ble/emo (MIT).
